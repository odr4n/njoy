Ladder Link privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026

The short version

  • Ladder Link has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.
  • Your progress, stars and coins are saved on your phone.
  • If you're signed in to Google Play Games, a backup of that progress is kept in your own Google account.
  • Coin purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

Who we are

Ladder Link is a daily word-ladder game made by n:joy studios, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean n:joy studios, and "the game" means the Ladder Link app for Android.

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email us at njoy.studios@nardo-tytherleigh.com.

Information stored on your phone

To remember your game, Ladder Link saves the following on your phone only:

  • which ladders you've finished, your times and your stars
  • your coin balance, daily-reward streak and boosts used
  • half-finished ladders, so you can carry on later
  • your settings, such as sound and daily reminders
  • the date you installed the game, which decides how far back your calendar goes

We can't see any of this. It's deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Cloud backup with Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the game backs up the progress listed above to your Google account using Google Play Games "saved games". The backup is stored in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so your progress and coins can be restored if you reinstall the game or move to a new phone.

The backup contains only game progress and settings. It doesn't contain your name, email address or contacts. It's handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and we don't run any servers of our own that receive it.

If you're not signed in to Play Games, nothing is backed up and the game works normally.

Buying coins

Coin packs are sold through Google Play's billing system. Google processes the payment, and your card or payment details are never shared with us. To give you your coins, the game receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play: which pack was bought, an order ID and the purchase time. We use it only to add the coins to your game and to check that the purchase is genuine.

Purchases are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The game uses Unity's In-App Purchasing tools to talk to Google Play. See the Unity privacy policy for game players.

Reminders

If you choose to turn them on, the game shows a daily reminder notification. These reminders are scheduled on your phone by the game itself. No information is sent anywhere to make them work. You can turn them off at any time in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Technical information

Ladder Link is built with the Unity game engine. Like most apps made with Unity, the engine may send Unity limited technical information about your device, such as the phone model, operating system version and screen size, to help keep the engine working well. This doesn't include your name, contacts or messages. It's handled by Unity under the Unity privacy policy for game players.

The game uses the internet for three things only: checking today's date by reading the time from a Google web address (so the daily ladder unlocks fairly), talking to Google Play for purchases and backups, and the Unity technical information above.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We don't show adverts or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

Ladder Link is a general-audience word game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Your choices and rights

If you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, data protection law (UK GDPR and the EU GDPR) gives you rights over your personal information, including the right to ask what we hold, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to a data protection authority. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office. People in other places, such as California, may have similar rights.

Because we don't run servers that store your information, most of it is under your control:

  • Information on your phone: uninstall the game, or clear its data in your phone's settings.
  • The cloud backup: in the Google Drive app or website, open Settings, then Manage apps, find Ladder Link and choose "Delete hidden app data".
  • Purchase records: these are kept by Google. You can see them in your Google Play order history.

If you need help with any of this, email njoy.studios@nardo-tytherleigh.com.

How long information is kept

Game data stays on your phone until you uninstall the game or clear its data. The cloud backup stays in your Google account until you delete it. Google keeps purchase records for as long as its own policies require.

Security

Information sent between the game and Google Play or Unity is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transfer is perfectly secure, but we keep the information involved to the minimum the game needs.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game uses information, for example by adding analytics, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live in the game.
